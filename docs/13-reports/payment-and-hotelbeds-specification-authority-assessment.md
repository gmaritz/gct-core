### Certification workflow and evidence

- **Confirmed by current document:** Architect-approved [INT-003 sections 10–20](../08-integration/INT-003-hotelbeds-real-world-integration-certification-verification.md) requires controlled TEST Availability → selected rate → CheckRate **only for `RECHECK`** → Booking → confirmation, then booking details where implemented and voucher/certification evidence. `BOOKABLE` bypasses unnecessary CheckRate; repeated Availability is not a substitute. Its checks include holder/paxes, child ages, room, rate, supplier reference/status/amount and persisted Reservation fulfilment. Normal automated tests must not make live supplier calls.
- **Confirmed by current document:** [HBX-001](../08-integration/hotelbeds/HBX-001-certification-readiness.md) is the recorded supplier control and evidence matrix. It records internal and controlled supplier evidence separately from formal certification; it calls for a booking confirmation timeout of at least 60 seconds and voucher/cancellation evidence where applicable. These are recorded requirements/status, not proof the current environment meets them. External Hotelbeds requirements can change and must be rechecked before submission.
- **Missing or not demonstrated:** HBX-001 marks multi-room allocation `CONDITIONAL`, records one controlled supplier transaction but says combined booking lifecycle and certification are not complete; remaining supplier semantics and the recorded Availability-to-Booking price difference require confirmation. Neither that evidence nor catalogue persistence establishes end-to-end production booking persistence.
