# Objective

Implement the canonical Pricing Policy Framework.

The framework evaluates commercial pricing policies and produces an immutable PricingStrategySet describing which pricing strategies apply.

Policies select commercial strategies.

They never perform pricing calculations.

# Policy Architecture

```text
Pricing Request

        │

        ▼

Pricing Policy Pipeline

        │

        ▼

Pricing Strategy Set

        │

        ▼

Pricing Calculator Framework
```

Policies determine strategy.

Calculators determine price.

---

# Pricing Strategy Set

Create:

```typescript
PricingStrategySet
```

Represents the immutable collection of pricing strategies selected for a pricing request.

Examples:

- Corporate pricing
- Seasonal pricing
- Promotional pricing
- Loyalty pricing
- Commission profile
- Markup profile
- Tax profile

The strategy set contains no calculated monetary values.

---

# Pricing Policy Context

Provides immutable context including:

- pricing request
- journey summary
- traveller information
- commercial metadata
- market
- sales channel
- booking date

---

# Pricing Policy Result

Represents the outcome of an individual policy evaluation.

Contains:

- policy name
- outcome
- selected strategy
- warnings
- metadata

Immutable.

---

# Pricing Policy

Create:

```typescript
PricingPolicy
```

Responsibilities:

- evaluate context
- determine applicability
- return PricingPolicyResult

Policies shall never:

- calculate prices
- modify aggregates
- invoke calculators
- call providers

---

# Policy Registry

Create:

```typescript
PricingPolicyRegistry
```

Responsibilities:

- register policies
- unregister policies
- resolve policies
- order by priority

Collections returned by the registry shall be immutable.

---

# Policy Pipeline

Create:

```typescript
PricingPolicyPipeline
```

Responsibilities:

- resolve registered policies
- execute in priority order
- aggregate policy results
- construct PricingStrategySet
- return immutable results

Critical denial policies may terminate execution early where appropriate.

# Dependency Injection

Policies shall be injected into the registry.

The pipeline shall receive the registry through constructor injection.

No policy shall instantiate another policy.

---

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a provider-independent Pricing Policy Framework that evaluates commercial pricing strategies and produces an immutable PricingStrategySet for consumption by the Pricing Calculator Framework.

The framework establishes a clear separation between commercial strategy selection and pricing calculation, ensuring that pricing policies remain declarative while calculators remain responsible for all monetary computation.
