# Objective

Implement the canonical Accommodation Content Service.

The service retrieves supplier-independent accommodation content through the Provider Capability Framework and returns immutable canonical content to the remainder of GCT Core.

The Accommodation Content Service is the authoritative application service responsible for accommodation content retrieval.

---

# Architectural Principles

Accommodation content belongs to GCT Core.

Providers supply content.

The Content Service orchestrates provider interaction.

The Content Service never exposes provider models.

The Content Service never performs business enrichment.

The Content Service never performs AI enhancement.

The Content Service never performs pricing or availability.

# Canonical Content

The service shall retrieve only

- accommodation name
- description
- images
- amenities
- location
- contacts
- policies
- rating
- provider reference

No pricing.

No availability.

No booking information.

---

# Aggregation

Aggregation shall remain structural.

The service shall never

- rank content
- rewrite descriptions
- merge text
- generate summaries
- optimise images
- infer business rules

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a supplier-independent Accommodation Content Service capable of retrieving immutable canonical accommodation content from one or more providers through the Provider Capability Framework.

The service establishes the definitive content retrieval capability for the Accommodation Engine and provides the foundation for accommodation detail pages, the Homepage Showcase, Package Builder, Journey Designer and future merchandising capabilities.

By separating canonical content retrieval from availability, rates, pricing and AI enhancement, this milestone ensures that GCT Core maintains a clean, extensible architecture while preparing the platform for future multilingual content, intelligent merchandising and multi-provider accommodation strategies.
