# Objective

Implement the canonical Payment Policy Framework.

The framework evaluates business policies after successful validation and before payment processing.

Policies determine **whether** and **how** payment processing may proceed.

Policies never perform payment processing.

---

# Policy Outcomes

The framework supports three canonical outcomes.

## ALLOW

Processing may continue.

---

## DENY

Processing stops immediately.

---

## REQUIRE_ACTION

Processing pauses until a required business action has been completed.

Examples:

- Strong Customer Authentication
- Manual Approval
- Fraud Review
- Identity Verification
- Compliance Review

REQUIRE_ACTION is **not** a validation failure.

---

# Policy Priority

Policies execute in deterministic priority order.

Example:

```text
CRITICAL

↓

HIGH

↓

NORMAL

↓

LOW
```

---

# Policy Registry

Responsibilities:

- register()
- unregister()
- resolve()
- resolveAll()

Rules:

- duplicate policy names rejected
- immutable resolved collections
- deterministic ordering

---

# Policy Pipeline

Responsibilities:

- resolve policies
- execute by priority
- short-circuit on DENY
- return immutable results

The pipeline never performs business processing.

---

# Policy Families

## Payment Eligibility Policy

Determines whether a reservation is eligible for payment.

---

## Payment Method Policy

Determines whether the selected payment method is permitted.

---

## Currency Policy

Determines whether settlement currency is supported.

---

## Settlement Policy

Determines whether settlement is permitted under current business rules.

---

## Authorization Policy

Determines authorization strategy.

Examples:

- immediate authorization
- delayed authorization
- pre-authorization

Framework only.

No provider logic.

---

## Refund Policy

Determines applicable refund strategy.

No refund execution occurs.

---

## Risk Policy

Determines whether additional business controls apply.

Examples:

- fraud review
- manual approval
- identity verification

Framework only.

---

# Policy Context

Each policy receives an immutable:

```typescript
PaymentPolicyContext
```

Containing:

- reservation snapshot
- pricing snapshot
- payment request
- payment method
- payment metadata

Policies may read context only.

---

# Policy Result

Each policy returns:

```typescript
PaymentPolicyResult
```

Containing:

- outcome
- required actions
- warnings
- metadata

Results are immutable.

---

# Constructor Injection

The pipeline receives the registry through constructor injection.

Policies are never instantiated internally.

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a deterministic, provider-independent Payment Policy Framework that evaluates payment eligibility, settlement rules and business controls through immutable, stateless policies before payment processing begins.

The framework establishes the canonical decision-making layer of the Payments Capability while remaining independent of payment gateways and payment execution.
