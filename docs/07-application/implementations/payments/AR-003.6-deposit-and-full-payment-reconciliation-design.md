## 2. Decision and scope

B01-B04 approve a component-derived initial requirement and the specified customer collection rules. Section 9.1 contains proposed APP-004.4 wording for review; it is not an amendment to that specification.

**B02 — APPROVED, Option A, 2026-11-17:** Tour/package commercial business logic owns the authoritative calculation. APP-004.4 evaluates its result; Reservation preserves the agreed terms; Payment processes an individual financial transaction; application orchestration coordinates the handoff.

**B03 — APPROVED, 2026-11-17:** Collect 100% of each selected Hotelbeds accommodation rate at booking regardless of refundability. Preserve supplier cancellation deadline, penalty and payment conditions separately. Cancellation rights do not reduce customer collection.

**Confirmed accommodation pricing arrangement:** Hotelbeds rates received by GCT Core already include Go Cape Tours' 10% accommodation profit margin. The received rate is the commercially applicable accommodation amount; GCT Core does not add another 10% accommodation markup. The same arrangement applies in production as in the current integration environment. This accommodation margin is not the overall package profit margin.

**B04 — APPROVED, 2026-11-17:** For multi-day tours and packages, collect 50% of the Go Cape Tours component if booking is more than 14 days before departure; collect 100% at exactly 14 days or fewer. Any remaining 50% is contractually due 14 days before departure. For private day tours, use the same 50%/100% rule at a 7-day threshold, with any remaining 50% due 7 days before the tour. Both percentages are configurable commercial policy, not architectural constants.

`PARTIAL_PAYMENT` and `FULL_PAYMENT` classify the **commercial requirement calculated before payment**. They are not Payment Aggregate lifecycle statuses or evidence that money has been received. The actual verified outcome of an individual Payment is a separate fact. An individual completed Payment does not, by itself, prove that the entire Reservation obligation is settled.

The initial online transaction still requires verification, correlation and reconciliation. Subsequent online balance collection, automated balance requests, invoice generation or updates, reminders, accounts-receivable management and manual-payment reconciliation after that transaction are excluded from this agreed scope. A historical contractual balance and due date do not authorise those capabilities or establish the current external balance.

D01 and D04 remain approved with their recorded conditions; D02 remains approved in principle with its conditions. D01 applies to the existing scoped first **full-payment** flow and is not automatically extended to partial payment. D03 and D05-D11 remain pending. B01-B04 do not decide D05, payment-attempt cardinality, confirmation details, the physical model or a repository API. ## 4. Required semantic contract

The following describes the approved business direction and the minimum information the eventual reviewed contracts must convey. It is **not** a proposed schema, API, named type, new Aggregate Root or approved storage format.

1. **Calculation and handoff:** The B02-authorised tour/package commercial business logic evaluates the individual booking and determines its positive amount payable now and commercial requirement classification, taking the applicable rules, selected components and supplier conditions into account. Application orchestration coordinates the calculated result, Reservation and Payment; APP-004.4 consumes and evaluates the result as policy. The detailed contract location, representation and handoff/version binding remain decisions.
2. **Historical terms:** At the point the customer agrees and the Reservation is established, preserve the applicable commercial terms and enough relevant calculation basis, selected-component and supplier-condition information to explain that agreement historically. Preserve applicable due dates without inferring an automated collection obligation. Exact historical data, precision, timestamps and retention remain to be specified.
3. **Distinct facts:** The agreed Reservation total, amount payable at booking, amount actually received, contractual future balance and due date, and verified outcome of an individual Payment are different facts. For the simple initial agreement with no other credits or adjustments, the contractual remainder is the agreed total less the amount payable at booking; treatment of pre-existing credits, amendments, refunds and adjustments is not decided here. A contractual remainder is not evidence that the initial transaction succeeded, nor is it necessarily today's externally maintained balance.
4. **Requirement classification:** Before payment, `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` means a positive amount less than the full Reservation price is payable at booking; `FULL_PAYMENT` means the full price is payable at booking. Neither classification is a Payment lifecycle status or evidence of receipt. Zero payment is not an allowed outcome. Invalid or uncalculable input must not be reclassified as a permitted zero-payment outcome; detailed failure handling and exact monetary rules remain open.
5. **Confirmation:** An authoritative verified outcome satisfying the historically agreed initial requirement may support Reservation confirmation, subject to the applicable confirmation conditions. Partial initial payment can therefore coexist with an outstanding contractual balance; a completed Payment is not proof of full Reservation settlement. D05 and D09 must establish acceptable evidence, predecessor states, additional fulfilment/operational conditions and behavior on discrepancies.
6. **Changed terms:** Later changes to tour rules, supplier rates, conditions or package configuration must not silently recalculate the agreed historical requirement. Changes to agreed Reservation terms require an explicit amendment process; its authorisation, operation, timing, outcome and effects on an in-flight Payment remain undecided.
7. **One architecture:** Private day tours and packages use the same calculation-to-Reservation-to-Payment boundaries. The selected rules and components differ; the architectural decision is not to branch payment policy universally by product category.

### Provenance and retries

[AR-003.3 §9](AR-003.3-payment-aggregate-persistence-and-itn-reconciliation-design.md#9-reservation-snapshot-and-confirmation-coordination) proposes one Payment/outcome marker for its initial full-payment case. A single marker may also be sufficient for B01 only if a Reservation has at most one applicable successful online booking-journey outcome, duplicate delivery is recognised, and different verified outcomes are retained and escalated rather than silently credited. External balance handling alone does not require more markers. That cardinality and recovery behavior are **not approved** by B01. If multiple distinct outcomes, reversals or corrections must be applied, review a bounded provenance alternative; do not presume a general multi-payment platform.

AR-003.3's full-payment-only and “partial payment requires separate approval” recommendations describe its earlier proposal. B01 now approves partial initial payment in principle; detailed mechanics, confirmation and specification amendments remain unapproved. AR-003.3 itself remains unchanged as a historical draft.

## 5. Commercial calculation contract design

This is an implementation-independent business contract, not an API, service, persistence model or schema. The approved owner is tour/package commercial business logic (B02). APP-004.4 consumes and evaluates the resulting requirement as policy. Reservation preserves the customer-agreed result, and Payment processes the amount supplied for one individual transaction. Application orchestration coordinates these responsibilities; it does not replace the commercial calculation owner.

### 5.1 Reference journey and authoritative input

The [INT-004 Signature Cape Winelands journey](../../../08-integration/INT-004-cape-winelands-dynamic-availability-pricing.md#41-journey-itinerary) is a four-day itinerary with two accommodation stops: two nights in Stellenbosch and one night in Hermanus. The Go Cape Tours (GCT) group total is a separate touring component from selected Hotelbeds accommodation.

The commercial calculation must receive or have access to the following decision-relevant information:

| Input group | Required information | Authority and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Journey and occupancy | Journey/package identity; departure date; itinerary and accommodation stop identities/dates; participant count; relevant room occupancy. | Selected journey and agreed dates/selections. The exact departure instant and date/timezone semantics remain open. |
| Selected accommodation | For every selected rate: property, room and rate identity; package stop; check-in/out; occupancy; Hotelbeds rate amount and currency as received by GCT Core; refundability; cancellation deadline and penalty terms; supplier payment conditions/deadlines; provider identity and opaque references necessary for fulfilment. | APP-008 selected offer, with current conditions after any required approved revalidation. Hotelbeds `rateKey` remains opaque outside the adapter. The received rate already includes the 10% accommodation profit margin and is the commercially applicable accommodation amount; GCT Core does not add another 10% accommodation markup. Customer collection is 100% of that amount under B03 irrespective of cancellation conditions. The production environment uses the same commercial pricing arrangement as the current integration environment. |
| GCT commercial component | Applicable costing/rate basis; participant count; applicable per-person or group basis; agreed GCT component total; multi-day/package or private day-tour rule; policy/effective basis. | The authoritative approved GCT commercial costing source. The component is not multiplied by participant count again where the source already supplies a group total. |
| Booking-time context | Booking date/time and applicable commercial rule version/effective basis. | Required to apply B04's lead-time threshold and reproduce the agreed result. Exact instant/calendar interpretation remains open. |

The source repository contains no tour-costing Excel workbook in the inspected working tree. The current checked-in commercial representation for this journey is the participant-count/per-person/group-total table in [INT-004 §5](../../../08-integration/INT-004-cape-winelands-dynamic-availability-pricing.md#5-go-cape-tours-pricing), which calls those totals authoritative for that integration iteration. It does not satisfy the missing costing-source need.

For clarity, distinguish the Hotelbeds rate amount and currency as received by GCT Core from the commercially applicable accommodation amount: under the confirmed arrangement, these are the same amount, and that received rate already incorporates the 10% accommodation profit margin. GCT Core must not add another 10% to it. The final customer-facing package price remains subject to the established GCT pricing and quotation rules; this accommodation margin does not establish or imply the overall package profit margin. The commercial arrangement is identical in the current integration environment and production; environment-specific supplier currency or operational settings do not constitute a different margin model.

To make Excel-based commercial rates authoritative, the accountable commercial owner must identify the exact workbook/source, controlled version and effective date, approval, applicable rows/formulas, participant and occupancy basis, and how a selected result is bound to the agreed Reservation for reproducibility. The consumption/import and calculation mechanism remain unspecified here. No workbook values are invented or hardcoded by this review.

### 5.2 Rule evaluation and component aggregation

Apply the approved customer collection rules in this order conceptually:

1. Establish the selected Accommodation component amount for every selected Hotelbeds rate using the rate amount as received by GCT Core. That amount already includes the 10% accommodation profit margin and is commercially applicable without an additional GCT Core 10% accommodation markup. The customer amount payable at booking for each selection is 100% of that amount under B03. Preserve supplier cancellation rights, penalties, cancellation deadline and supplier payment conditions as separate facts; none changes that customer collection amount.
2. Obtain the agreed Go Cape Tours component total using its authoritative participant/group costing basis. Apply the relevant B04 rule to this component only:
   - Multi-day/package: more than 14 days before departure, 50% payable at booking and 50% contractually due 14 days before departure; at 14 days or fewer, 100% payable at booking and no future balance from this component.
   - Private day tour: more than 7 days before the tour, 50% payable at booking and 50% contractually due 7 days before the tour; at 7 days or fewer, 100% payable at booking and no future balance from this component.
3. Sum component amounts only after their currencies are compatible under an approved policy. No conversion, FX source, spread or rate is established by B01-B04. If amounts cannot be reconciled, the result must not assert a valid consolidated payable total; handling is an open contract question.
4. Compute the final customer-facing package price under the established APP-005 pricing and quotation rules, using each commercially applicable accommodation amount once alongside the agreed GCT component. The received Hotelbeds rate already includes the 10% accommodation profit margin; GCT Core must not apply that 10% again. Other pricing elements may apply only as specified by existing approved rules, and must not reapply this accommodation margin. The accommodation margin does not determine the overall package profit margin.
5. Compute total initial amount payable as full selected accommodation amount plus the B04-selected amount of the GCT component. The contractual future balance is any remaining GCT component share under B04, with the corresponding contractual due date. Do not treat this due date as an instruction to collect, invoice or remind.
6. Classify the commercial requirement before payment: `FULL_PAYMENT` when the initial amount equals the agreed Reservation total; otherwise `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` when it is positive and less than the total. The classification describes what is contractually due at booking, not a financial event. Zero-payment is prohibited by B01; inconsistent, zero, negative, over-total or unpriceable inputs need an approved fail-closed result contract.

This rule sequence does not define whether components are priced per person, room, stay or night; INT-004/APP-005 and the selected canonical rate basis remain controlling. Component aggregation, duplicate/missing selections and compatibility between supplier and customer currencies require detailed approval.

### 5.3 Conceptual result to orchestration

The business calculation returns a conceptual, immutable decision result for application orchestration to validate/coordinate. This list describes meaning only; it is not an interface or persistence contract.

| Result fact | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Requirement classification | `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` or `FULL_PAYMENT`, determined before payment; never a Payment status or receipt signal. |
| Reservation total and currency | Agreed customer total and its commercial currency. |
| Component totals and currencies | Total selected accommodation component, using Hotelbeds rate amounts as received (inclusive of the 10% accommodation profit margin), and agreed GCT touring component; preserve source currencies and do not aggregate incompatible currencies without approved reconciliation. |
| Amount payable at booking | Accommodation amount (100% under B03), GCT amount selected under B04, and total initial amount payable after currency compatibility is established. |
| Contractual future balance/due date | Remaining GCT component share and its due date when B04 applies; absent for these components when 100% is due at booking. |
| Component calculation basis | For each accommodation selection, stop, selected property/room/rate, stay, occupancy, amount/currency, opaque provider references, refundability, cancellation and supplier-payment conditions. For GCT, source costing identity/version/effective basis, participant/group basis, applicable rule and threshold evaluation. |
| Applicable commercial policy | Multi-day/package or private day-tour policy and effective basis used; 50% remains configurable rather than architectural. |

Keep the following facts distinct throughout downstream handling:

- **Reservation total:** the agreed customer price for the complete Reservation.
- **Amount payable at booking:** the contractual amount required initially by B03/B04.
- **Amount actually received:** money evidenced as received; it may differ from the amount due and is not populated merely by calculating a requirement.
- **Contractual future balance and due date:** the agreed remainder under the applicable policy, not a current receivable or authorization for later collection.
- **Individual Payment outcome:** provider-verified result for one transaction, matched to that transaction's own amount/currency; it is not automatically full Reservation settlement.

Historical preservation must bind the agreed calculation result to its inputs and commercial source basis sufficiently to prevent current rates/rules from silently changing the existing Reservation. Exact representation, source retention and reproducibility remain for approval.

## 6. Signature Cape Winelands worked examples

These are symbolic examples, not real supplier quotes or new costing values. They assume the selected accommodation rates and GCT component are denominated in one compatible customer currency solely to show the B03/B04 arithmetic. If actual supplier currencies differ, do not add them until an approved currency policy applies.

The four-day itinerary is Day 1–4 with two nights in Stellenbosch and one night in Hermanus. Let:

- `A_S` be the sum of the selected Hotelbeds rate amounts as received by GCT Core for the two-night Stellenbosch stop and all selected rooms/rates there. These amounts already include the 10% accommodation profit margin; no additional 10% is applied.
- `A_H` be the sum of the selected Hotelbeds rate amounts as received by GCT Core for the one-night Hermanus stop and all selected rooms/rates there. These amounts already include the 10% accommodation profit margin; no additional 10% is applied.
- `G(n)` be the authoritative Go Cape Tours component total for the actual participant count `n`, taken from the approved, effective commercial costing basis.
- `T = A_S + A_H + G(n)` be the Reservation total, only after currency compatibility is established.

Select a refundable Hotelbeds rate at one stop and a non-refundable rate at the other for illustration. Their actual cancellation deadlines and penalty conditions are retained separately with their respective rates. Under B03, the amount payable at booking for each remains 100% of its received, commercially applicable rate amount; neither refundability nor a cancellation deadline discounts the collection. This does not equate the 10% accommodation margin with the overall package profit margin.

### Scenario A — booking more than 14 days before departure

This is a multi-day package, so B04 applies the 14-day rule:

| Component | Reservation total | Payable at booking | Contractual future balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Stellenbosch accommodation (two nights) | `A_S` | `A_S` (100%) | None under this component |
| Hermanus accommodation (one night) | `A_H` | `A_H` (100%) | None under this component |
| Go Cape Tours component | `G(n)` | `0.50 × G(n)` | `0.50 × G(n)`, due 14 days before departure |
| Total | `T` | `A_S + A_H + 0.50 × G(n)` | `0.50 × G(n)`, contractually due 14 days before departure |

Assuming positive `G(n)` and compatible currency, the pre-payment requirement classification is `PARTIAL_PAYMENT`. This is not a Payment lifecycle status. Amount actually received remains unknown until a verified individual Payment outcome is recorded. Preserve each selected rate's refundability, cancellation terms and supplier payment terms separately.

### Scenario B — booking exactly 14 days before departure

The exact boundary falls within “14 days or fewer”; B04 requires the full GCT component:

| Component | Reservation total | Payable at booking | Contractual future balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Stellenbosch accommodation (two nights) | `A_S` | `A_S` (100%) | None under this component |
| Hermanus accommodation (one night) | `A_H` | `A_H` (100%) | None under this component |
| Go Cape Tours component | `G(n)` | `G(n)` (100%) | None under this component |
| Total | `T` | `T` | None from these components |

Assuming valid positive compatible component amounts, the pre-payment requirement classification is `FULL_PAYMENT`. This is not evidence that a Payment has been initiated or received. The refundable and non-refundable accommodation rates still have distinct preserved cancellation conditions, while both are collected at 100%.

The private day-tour threshold is separate: more than 7 days uses 50% of its GCT component at booking and a contractual remainder due 7 days before the tour; exactly 7 days or fewer uses 100%. The day-tour rule does not replace the package rule for this four-day journey.

## 9. Controlled proposed amendment text

Each block below identifies an insertion or qualification to the existing named document and section. It is **draft wording for review**, not an amendment to the referenced file. The referenced source retains its current identifier, version, revision history and stated status. The relevant owner must choose the next controlled version, record approval and revision history, and incorporate only the wording accepted for that document. Bracketed open decisions are not resolved by these drafts.

### 9.1 APP-004.4 - Reservation Payment Policy: proposed policy-consumer and business boundary wording

Insert in the Payment Policy section, subject to approval of the detailed upstream tour/package calculation contract and its location:

> The applicable tour and package business logic SHALL determine the commercial amount payable at booking dynamically for the individual booking, using applicable tour-specific rules and the selected package components and their commercial conditions. Inputs MAY include configurable deposit percentages, booking and commencement dates, supplier payment requirements, cancellation conditions and deadlines, refundable or non-refundable rates, and other applicable commercial conditions. No universal fixed deposit amount, universal percentage, or hardcoded day-tour/package distinction SHALL determine the outcome.
>
> The calculated commercial requirement classification, determined before payment, SHALL be `PARTIAL_PAYMENT`, where a positive amount less than the full Reservation price is payable at booking, or `FULL_PAYMENT`, where the full Reservation price is payable at booking. Neither classification is a Payment Aggregate lifecycle status or evidence that money has been received. A zero-payment or `NO_PAYMENT_REQUIRED` outcome SHALL NOT be produced.
>
> Under B04, for multi-day tours and packages, a booking more than 14 days before departure requires the applicable configurable 50% of the Go Cape Tours component at booking; the remaining 50%, where applicable, is contractually due 14 days before departure. A booking exactly 14 days before departure or closer requires 100% of that Go Cape Tours component at booking. For private day tours, apply the corresponding configurable 50% / 100% rule at a 7-day threshold, with any remaining 50% due 7 days before the tour. The 50% values are commercial configuration, not architectural constants.
>
> Under B03, collect 100% of the amount for every selected Hotelbeds accommodation rate at booking, whether refundable or non-refundable. Preserve that rate's actual cancellation deadline, penalty conditions and supplier payment conditions separately. Cancellation rights do not reduce the customer amount collected. Multiple selected rates contribute their full selected amounts; supplier payment timing is not the customer's contractual collection timing.
>
> The calculation result and basis SHALL be supplied to application orchestration for preservation as agreed Reservation terms and for determining the initial Payment amount. APP-004.4 Reservation Payment Policy SHALL consume and evaluate that result within its policy responsibilities; it is not the authoritative commercial calculation engine. Neither the Reservation Aggregate nor Payment Aggregate SHALL calculate supplier collection requirements or interpret live supplier cancellation policies. This contract does not by itself change the existing policy result shape or designate the policy pipeline as the calculator.

[Open: detailed calculation contract representation/handoff, component aggregation, currency compatibility, precision and rounding, lead-time calendar and timezone semantics, supplier-condition conflicts, historical basis, amendment handling and invalid/unavailable inputs.]

### 9.2 APP-004 - Reservation Capability: proposed addition to Capability Responsibilities

> Reservation orchestration coordinates the initial payment requirement calculated by the authoritative tour/package commercial business logic and preserves the agreed result as Reservation history. APP-004.4 evaluates the result within its policy responsibilities. Reservation does not own tour/package calculation or payment processing. Confirmation applies the separately approved Reservation policy.

### 9.3 APP-004.1 - proposed additions to Snapshot Principle and Reservation Status

> The Reservation SHALL preserve the customer's agreed initial payment requirement, relevant terms, calculation basis and applicable future contractual deadlines as immutable historical commercial state. Later changes to current tour, package or supplier rules SHALL NOT silently change agreed terms. A change to existing agreed terms requires an explicit Reservation amendment process.
>
> Reservation remains authoritative for its lifecycle. Subject to the approved confirmation contract and applicable fulfilment and operational conditions, a verified initial Payment satisfying a `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` requirement may support confirmation with a contractual balance remaining; a `FULL_PAYMENT` requirement is assessed against its own agreed amount. An individual Payment outcome alone SHALL NOT establish full Reservation settlement.

[Open D09: operation, predecessor states, exact evidence, fulfilment conditions, concurrency, failures and amendment interaction. Existing `CONFIRMED` state is not itself a new transition.]

### 9.4 APP-004.2 - proposed additions to Pricing Snapshot, Payment Snapshot and Versioning

> Preserve the approved initial requirement and the relevant historical basis and contractual deadlines alongside the agreed pricing history, using a representation approved by the affected specification owners. Preserve initial amount payable separately from the verified amount received and the contractual future balance. Identify the historical/as-of meaning of each value; do not represent externally managed later collection as current GCT payment activity.
>
> The approved snapshot contract SHALL support identifying the verified Payment outcome applied to the Reservation so duplicate application is detectable. Its exact provenance representation and any multiple-outcome policy require separate approval. Use the approved exact-money and currency contract. Explicitly amended terms create new historical state without silently rewriting the prior agreement.

[Open: exact fields, source references, history retention, event cardinality, monetary precision and migration/legacy handling. No JSON or table is authorised.]

### 9.5 APP-004.3 and APP-004.7 - proposed dependent qualifications

APP-004.3, Commercial Integrity Validator:

> Evaluate payment/Reservation compatibility against the preserved applicable requirement and approved confirmation policy. A positive contractual balance alone SHALL NOT be treated as incompatible with a properly confirmed Reservation whose approved partial initial payment requirement is satisfied.

APP-004.7, Reservation Presentation Model:

### 9.6 APP-006 and provider-neutral integration - proposed qualifications

APP-006, Core Components, and APP-006.1, Aggregate Responsibilities:

> Payment represents an individual financial transaction for an authoritative amount supplied by application orchestration from the agreed requirement. It SHALL NOT calculate tour deposits, interpret live supplier terms, or represent a completed individual transaction as proof that the whole Reservation obligation is settled.

APP-006.2, Lifecycle:

> Canonical Payment status and evidence describe the individual transaction; Reservation confirmation and overall obligation satisfaction are separate decisions. The mapping of a provider-reported outcome to canonical lifecycle stages remains subject to the approved lifecycle contract.
> `PARTIAL_PAYMENT` and `FULL_PAYMENT` are pre-payment commercial requirement classifications, not Payment lifecycle statuses or evidence of receipt.

APP-006.3, Validation Stages:

> Validate the initial Payment amount against the applicable preserved agreed requirement, the approved currency and monetary contract, rather than assuming it must equal the Reservation total. Reject missing, invalid or inconsistent authoritative requirements without substituting a zero amount.

APP-006.4, Policy Context:

> Payment processing policies consume the applicable agreed requirement through the approved context. They determine payment-processing permission and strategy; they do not calculate the tour/package commercial obligation.

APP-006.8, PaymentGatewayRequest:

> The request amount is the authorised individual transaction amount supplied by the application, not necessarily the total Reservation price. The gateway SHALL NOT calculate the commercial requirement.

These qualifications do not select a D05 transition, create intermediate evidence, or approve a new Payment API.
