# Objective

Implement the canonical Pricing Aggregate.

The Pricing Aggregate represents the immutable commercial valuation of a travel product.

It owns the complete pricing outcome but performs no calculations.

Pricing calculations are delegated to the Pricing Calculator Framework.

# Aggregate Architecture

The Pricing Aggregate owns the immutable commercial pricing outcome.

```text
Pricing Breakdown

        │

        ▼

Pricing Aggregate

        │

        ▼

Pricing Result
```

The aggregate never performs calculations.

---

# Aggregate Responsibilities

The Pricing Aggregate owns:

- pricing identity
- pricing summary
- pricing breakdown
- tax summary
- fee summary
- discount summary
- markup summary
- commission summary
- pricing totals
- currency
- pricing metadata

The aggregate is the authoritative pricing record.

---

# Aggregate Invariants

Construction shall fail if:

- pricing identifier is missing
- currency is missing
- pricing totals are missing
- pricing breakdown is missing
- metadata is missing

The aggregate shall never exist in a partially constructed state.

---

# Immutability

The aggregate shall expose:

- readonly properties
- readonly collections
- defensive copies where required

No mutable references may escape the aggregate.

---

# Construction

Provide:

```typescript
Pricing.create(...)
```

Responsibilities:

- validate construction invariants
- create immutable aggregate
- initialise aggregate state

---

# Restoration

Provide:

```typescript
Pricing.restore(...)
```

Responsibilities:

- recreate aggregate from persisted state
- preserve immutability
- avoid recalculation
