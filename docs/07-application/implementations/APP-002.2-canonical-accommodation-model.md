# Objective

Implement the canonical Accommodation Model used by the Accommodation Engine.

The Accommodation Model represents the business concept of accommodation within GCT Core.

It is completely independent of supplier APIs.

All supplier integrations shall map to this model.

---

# Architectural Principle

Accommodation belongs to GCT Core.

Suppliers provide accommodation data.

They do not define the Accommodation Model.

The canonical model becomes the permanent business representation of accommodation.

Create

