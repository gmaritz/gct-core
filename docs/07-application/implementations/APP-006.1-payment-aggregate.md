# Objective

Implement the canonical Payment Aggregate.

The aggregate represents the immutable commercial settlement record of GCT Core.

It owns all payment state and snapshots required throughout the payment lifecycle.

# Aggregate Architecture

```text
Payment

    │

    ├── Reservation Snapshot

    ├── Quote Snapshot

    ├── Pricing Snapshot

    ├── Payment Method

    ├── Authorization

    ├── Capture

    ├── Settlement

    ├── Refunds

    ├── Timeline

    └── Metadata
```

The aggregate owns immutable copies of every commercial settlement artefact.

---

# Aggregate Responsibilities

The Payment Aggregate owns:

- payment identity
- reservation reference
- quote reference
- pricing snapshot
- payment amount
- currency
- payment method
- payment status
- authorization snapshot
- capture snapshot
- settlement snapshot
- refund history
- payment timeline
- metadata

---

# Aggregate Invariants

Construction shall fail when:

- payment identifier missing
- reservation snapshot missing
- pricing snapshot missing
- payment method missing
- currency missing
- payment status missing
- metadata missing


---

Provide:

```typescript
Payment.restore(...)
```

Restores an immutable aggregate from persistence.

No validation logic beyond invariant enforcement.

---

# Ownership Model

The aggregate owns immutable snapshots of:

- Reservation
- Quote (optional)
- Pricing
- Authorization
- Capture
- Settlement
- Refunds
- Timeline

Snapshots are immutable.

---

# Lifecycle

Canonical payment lifecycle:

```text
Created

↓

PendingAuthorization

↓

Authorized

↓

Captured

↓

Settled

↓

Completed
```

Alternative flows:

```text
Created

↓

AuthorizationFailed

↓

Cancelled
```

```text
Captured

↓

RefundRequested

↓

Refunded
```

Future stages such as PartialCapture, PartialRefund and Chargeback may extend the lifecycle without changing the aggregate contract.

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a provider-independent immutable Payment Aggregate that represents the canonical commercial settlement record of the platform.

The aggregate establishes the foundation of the Payments Capability by owning immutable payment state while remaining independent of processing, presentation and provider implementations.
