# Objective

Implement the canonical Reservation Aggregate.

The Reservation Aggregate represents the commercial booking contract between Go Cape Tours and the traveller.

It is the Aggregate Root for all reservation-related business operations.

The aggregate shall be immutable by design and shall preserve historical booking information through snapshots rather than live references.

# Aggregate Composition

The Reservation Aggregate shall compose the following canonical business objects.

```text
Reservation

├── ReservationIdentity

├── ReservationStatus

├── JourneySnapshot

├── TravellerSnapshots

├── AccommodationSnapshots

├── PricingSnapshot

├── PaymentSnapshot

├── SupplierReferences

├── ReservationTimeline

└── ReservationMetadata
```

Each component shall be immutable.

---

# Reservation Identity

The aggregate shall expose a unique ReservationIdentity.

Identity is immutable.

Identity shall never change during the reservation lifecycle.

---

# Reservation Status

The aggregate shall expose ReservationStatus.

The lifecycle model will be implemented in APP-004.2.

The aggregate shall support the status concept only.

---

# Snapshot Principle

The Reservation Aggregate shall never depend upon live business aggregates.

Instead, it shall own immutable snapshots.

Examples

- JourneySnapshot
- TravellerSnapshot
- AccommodationSnapshot
- PricingSnapshot
- PaymentSnapshot

Snapshots preserve the historical state of the booking at the time it was created or amended.

---

# Timeline

The aggregate shall contain a ReservationTimeline.

Purpose

Record significant business milestones.

Examples

- Created
- Quoted
- Confirmed
- Amended
- Cancelled
- Completed

Timeline behaviour will be introduced in later milestones.

---

# Metadata

The aggregate shall expose immutable ReservationMetadata.

Typical information

- createdAt
- updatedAt
- version

Additional metadata may be introduced later without changing the aggregate contract.

---

# Responsibilities

The Reservation Aggregate shall

- represent a commercial reservation
- own reservation snapshots
- expose immutable business state
- enforce aggregate integrity

The Reservation Aggregate shall never

- perform orchestration
- call providers
- calculate pricing
- execute validation
- evaluate policies

---

# Aggregate Invariants

The aggregate shall always contain

- ReservationIdentity
- ReservationStatus
- JourneySnapshot
- At least one TravellerSnapshot
- ReservationMetadata

Construction shall fail if required components are absent.

---

# Immutability

The aggregate shall expose

- readonly properties
- readonly collections
- immutable composition

The aggregate shall never expose mutable internal state.
