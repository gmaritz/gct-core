# Objective

Implement the canonical Reservation Policy Framework.

The framework evaluates whether a structurally valid reservation is permitted to proceed through the commercial booking lifecycle.

Policies determine commercial permission.

They do not perform validation.

# Policy Architecture

The Reservation Policy Pipeline coordinates specialised policy families.

```text
Reservation Validation Result

        │

        ▼

Reservation Policy Context

        │

        ▼

Eligibility Policy

        │

        ▼

Commercial Policy

        │

        ▼

Supplier Policy

        │

        ▼

Payment Policy

        │

        ▼

Reservation Policy Result
```

Each policy evaluates one commercial concern.

---

# Reservation Policy Context

Create

```typescript
ReservationPolicyContext
```

Purpose

Provide a single immutable context shared by every reservation policy.

The context shall be created once and reused throughout policy evaluation.

---

# Reservation Policy Result

Create

```typescript
ReservationPolicyResult
```

Contains

- permitted
- outcome
- errors
- warnings
- observations
- metadata

Immutable.

Conforms to APP-001D.

---

# Reservation Policy Registry

Create

```typescript
ReservationPolicyRegistry
```

Responsibilities

- register policies
- unregister policies
- resolve policies
- resolve all policies
- return policies ordered by priority
- reject duplicate registration

Collections returned shall be immutable.

---

# Reservation Policy Pipeline

Responsibilities

- resolve registered policies
- execute by priority
- short-circuit on critical denial
- aggregate policy results
- return immutable ReservationPolicyResult

The pipeline shall coordinate policies only.

---

# Policy Families

## Reservation Eligibility Policy

Evaluates whether the reservation may proceed.

Examples

- reservation status
- mandatory commercial prerequisites
- traveller eligibility

---

## Reservation Commercial Policy

Evaluates commercial rules.

Examples

- pricing consistency
- quotation validity
- booking expiry
- commercial constraints

---

## Reservation Supplier Policy

Evaluates supplier-related rules.

Examples

- supplier availability
- supplier references
- supplier restrictions

The initial implementation defines the contract only.

---

## Reservation Payment Policy

Evaluates payment-related rules.

Examples

- deposit requirements
- outstanding balances
- payment status compatibility

The initial implementation defines the contract only.

---

## Reservation Amendment Policy

Defines the extension point for future reservation amendments.

Interface only.

---

## Reservation Cancellation Policy

Defines the extension point for future cancellation workflows.

Interface only.

---

# Policy Execution

Policies shall

- execute deterministically
- be stateless
- be immutable
- receive ReservationPolicyContext
- return ReservationPolicyResult

Policies shall never

- execute validation
- call providers
- persist data
- construct reservations

---

# Dependency Injection

Inject

- ReservationPolicyRegistry

The pipeline shall never instantiate policies directly.

---

# Failure Behaviour

Policy denials are business outcomes.

Business policy failures shall never throw exceptions.

Unexpected technical failures may raise exceptions.

Critical denials terminate policy execution immediately.

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a canonical Reservation Policy Framework that evaluates commercial permission through specialised, immutable, stateless policy families coordinated by a reusable policy pipeline.

The implementation establishes the commercial decision layer of the Reservation Capability, clearly separating validation of reservation correctness from authorization to proceed through the booking lifecycle.
