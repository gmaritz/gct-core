# Objective

Implement the canonical Accommodation Inventory Service.

The service retrieves supplier-independent accommodation inventory information through the Provider Capability Framework and returns immutable canonical inventory results.

Inventory includes accommodation availability and room inventory information only.

The service shall not perform pricing or booking operations.

---

# Architectural Principles

Inventory belongs to GCT Core.

Providers expose inventory.

The Inventory Service orchestrates provider execution.

The Inventory Service never exposes provider models.

The Inventory Service never performs pricing.

The Inventory Service never performs booking.

The Inventory Service conforms to APP-001A.

# Application Service Pattern

The service shall implement the canonical application service contract.

```typescript
ApplicationService<
    AccommodationInventoryQuery,
    AccommodationAvailabilityResult
>
```

The public operation shall be

```typescript

    query
)
```

No additional public operations shall exist.

# Inventory Philosophy

Inventory describes accommodation availability only.

The service shall never

- calculate prices
- calculate discounts
- create reservations
- allocate rooms
- perform booking

Those belong to later services.

---

# Aggregation

Aggregation remains structural.

No ranking.

No optimisation.

No business decisions.

---

# Error Handling

Provider failures remain isolated.

Use

```typescript
Promise.allSettled()
```

Successful providers continue returning inventory.

# Future Evolution

The Inventory Service is intentionally designed to support future enhancements without changing its public API.

Future capabilities include:

- room-level inventory
- occupancy validation
- room allocation
- inventory caching
- supplier inventory reconciliation
- inventory freshness policies
- partner-specific inventory rules

These enhancements shall extend the service without changing its public contract.

---

# Milestone Outcome

Upon successful completion, GCT Core shall expose a supplier-independent Accommodation Inventory Service that conforms to the APP-001A Application Service Pattern and retrieves canonical accommodation inventory through the Provider Capability Framework.

The service establishes the definitive inventory capability for the Accommodation Engine while remaining completely independent of pricing, booking and reservation logic. Together with the Discovery and Content services, it completes the core accommodation business capabilities required by the forthcoming Accommodation Rate Service and Package Builder.
