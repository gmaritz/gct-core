## 1. Executive findings

- **Unresolved - Hotelbeds source bounds:** The repository contains no authoritative Hotelbeds field-level limit for monetary amount, integral digits, fractional scale, or permitted JSON representation. The local DTOs and mapper behavior are implementation evidence, not supplier guarantees.
- **Unresolved - General GCT multiplicities:** Generic accommodation and package models do not establish maximum stops, nights, rooms, adults, children, or supplier-price aggregate size. A defensible platform-wide accommodation multiplier or commercial total cannot be calculated.
- **Verified - Cape Winelands exception:** Its application resolver defines two stops of two and one nights. The server-side booking-context validator enforces 2-8 adults, at least one adult in every room, the same adult party at both stops, and no children under the default journey policy. This yields at most 8 rooms per stop and 16 room-rate inputs over the journey.
- **Derived - Cape Winelands price sum:** The maximum number of selected room-rate addends is 16. The journey's nights do not multiply these rates: the availability mapper marks the selected rate `TOTAL_STAY`. No per-rate monetary maximum is established, so this does not establish a total-price maximum.
- **Observed - Current representation:** Hotelbeds monetary values and the principal pricing paths become JavaScript `number`. Reservation snapshots are JSON reconstructed with `Number`; relational amounts commonly use `Decimal(19,4)`. Neither the supplier limits nor current application conversion provide an exact end-to-end bound.
- **Unresolved - Precision configuration:** No `decimal.js` dependency/configuration or enforced general input/operation limits were found. Existing evidence is insufficient to select and validate a precision/resource bound for the general supplier and package paths.

The report does not reopen Decisions 1A or 2, make a commercial decision, or add an accommodation margin. It treats the mapped Hotelbeds rate as the supplier amount; the previously approved rule that the supplier rate already includes the 10% accommodation margin remains unchanged.

The approved currency scope is retained: ZAR is the production commercial and PayFast currency; EUR supplier input is for development/testing only. No currency support or conversion behavior is proposed here.

### 3.1 Verified aggregation derivation

For Cape Winelands:

1. The configured stop count is 2 (`CAPE_WINELANDS_STOPS`).
2. Each stop has exactly one room allocation. The participant count is 2-8, and each room must contain at least one adult; each stop's adult total must equal the participant count.
3. Therefore room count is 1-8 per stop, and 2-16 room allocations across both stops.
4. The selected rate amount is explicitly `TOTAL_STAY`; the current calculation does not multiply it by the 2/1-night stay. `src/application/accommodation/providers/hotelbeds/mapper/availability-response.mapper.ts:L199-L231`.
5. `selectJourneyAccommodation` sums the selected per-room rates; pricing then adds one accommodation line per stop. `src/application/journeys/models/experience/journey-accommodation.ts:L80-L146`; `src/application/pricing/accommodation/accommodation-pricing-input.ts:L50-L88`, `L158-L165`.
6. Consequently, 16 is a defensible maximum number of selected room-rate addends for this journey. It is not a bound on the amount of an addend or on the final commercial total.

The Cape group amount is selected by participant count, with a maximum configured table value of ZAR 115,680. The total would still depend on the sum of supplier room prices and, for EUR testing input, the configured conversion rate. Neither the supplier amount nor the FX rate has an established upper bound. No defensible maximum Cape Winelands quote amount can therefore be derived.

For the generic platform, at least one required limit is missing in every candidate aggregation: supplier amount maximum, stop count, room count, stay length, participant/occupancy bounds, or FX rate bound. No generic maximum multiplier or aggregate amount is calculated.

## 5. Evidence gaps and recommendations

### Evidence gaps

1. **Hotelbeds:** Obtain the official field-level schema or written confirmation answering the questions in §2.2, with document revision and section/page references. In particular, determine permitted JSON number/string representation, max amount, integral digits, fractional scale, and currency semantics for every consumed monetary field.
2. **Generic GCT accommodation:** Define and enforce general maximum rooms/groups, adults and children per room and journey, stop count, and nights per stay/journey if a universal aggregation bound is required. Existing generic checks validate some positive/integer shape but do not establish maxima.
3. **Cape Winelands:** Supplier amount and FX-rate bounds are missing even though room-rate term count is bounded. The group table does not bound the combined total.
4. **Exact representation:** Establish how supplier decimal text is retained through parsing, pricing, snapshots and persistence. Existing Number-based parsing and JSON reconstruction prevent an exactness guarantee.
5. **Persistence:** Establish application-level bounds and round-trip tests for each Decimal column and JSON snapshot separately. Checked-in migrations do not prove deployed database state.
6. **Resource limits:** Establish maximum decimal input length/scale, maximum aggregation count, and permitted operation complexity before validating any decimal precision configuration. No current general bounds support that validation.

### Recommendations to the System Architect

- Keep Decision 1B conditional pending authoritative Hotelbeds monetary constraints and application multiplicity bounds; this report makes no final approval decision.
- Assess supplier source values, FX/intermediates, commercial totals, snapshots, and relational payment amounts as separate contracts. Do not infer one category's bound from another.
- Treat the Cape Winelands 16-rate-input result as journey-specific only. Do not promote it to a platform maximum.
- For any proposed `Decimal(19,4)` use, establish a maximum amount and aggregate range that fit the column, prove scale preservation at the relevant boundary, and verify database/application reconstruction.
- Do not infer an amount cap from fixtures, the 19,4 schema, PayFast's safe-cent limit, or the Cape group-component lookup.
