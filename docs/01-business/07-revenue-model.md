# GCT Core – Revenue Model

**Project:** GCT Core (Go Cape Tours Core Platform)
**Document:** Revenue Model
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 17 July 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

This document defines how GCT Core generates revenue.

Unlike traditional online travel agencies that compete primarily on volume, GCT Core generates revenue through premium travel planning, curated experiences, accommodation partnerships and personalised customer service.

The objective is to maximise customer value rather than maximise transaction volume.

This document defines the commercial revenue model independently of pricing calculations or technical implementation.

---

# 2. Revenue Philosophy

Revenue should be generated by delivering exceptional value to customers.

The platform is designed around long-term customer relationships rather than one-time transactions.

Every booking should create value for:

- The traveller
- Go Cape Tours
- Supplier partners

Long-term sustainability is prioritised over short-term revenue.

---

# 3. Primary Revenue Streams

## Accommodation

Accommodation represents one of the primary revenue streams.

Revenue may be generated through:

- Supplier commissions
- Net-rate agreements
- Preferred supplier arrangements
- Dynamic package pricing

Accommodation also creates opportunities for additional curated experiences.

---

## Private Experiences

Go Cape Tours' privately curated experiences represent a core source of revenue.

Examples include:

- Private wine experiences
- Cape Peninsula experiences
- Cape Town experiences
- Garden Route experiences
- Multi-day journeys

Revenue is generated through professional planning, expert guiding and premium service.

---

## Wine Experiences

Wine tourism is a specialist area of the business.

Revenue may be generated through:

- Premium wine experiences
- Bespoke wine itineraries
- Exclusive estate partnerships
- Private cellar visits
- Fine dining experiences

Wine tourism remains one of the strongest differentiators of the Go Cape Tours brand.

---

## Curated Travel Packages

Packages combine multiple services into a single customer journey.

Packages may include:

- Accommodation
- Private experiences
- Wine experiences
- Destination experiences

Packages increase customer convenience while increasing overall booking value.

---

## Bespoke Itinerary Planning

Customers may purchase fully customised travel itineraries.

Revenue is generated through:

- Professional travel planning
- Local expertise
- Journey design
- Personal consultation
- Premium service

This represents one of the highest-value offerings within GCT Core.

---

# 4. Secondary Revenue Opportunities

The platform should remain flexible enough to support future revenue opportunities including:

- Exclusive travel collections
- Seasonal experiences
- Luxury events
- Private hosted journeys
- Corporate incentive travel
- Specialist travel themes

These opportunities should extend the platform without requiring architectural changes.

---

# 5. Revenue Diversification

Revenue should never depend on a single supplier, destination or experience.

Diversification reduces commercial risk while improving resilience.

Diversification should occur across:

- Destinations
- Accommodation partners
- Experience partners
- Customer markets
- Seasonal offerings

---

# 6. Revenue Principles

The following principles guide commercial decisions.

## Customer Value

Revenue should be earned by delivering measurable customer value.

---

## Premium Positioning

The platform competes through quality, expertise and personalised service rather than price.

---

## Sustainable Growth

Commercial growth should preserve the long-term reputation of Go Cape Tours.

---

## Transparency

Pricing presented to customers should always be clear and transparent.

---

## Commercial Integrity

Supplier relationships should remain ethical, sustainable and mutually beneficial.

---

# 7. Customer Lifetime Value

The objective is to develop long-term customer relationships.

Revenue should be generated through:

- Repeat journeys
- Expanded itineraries
- Destination progression
- Seasonal travel
- Customer referrals

The lifetime value of a customer is significantly more important than the value of a single booking.

---

# 8. Business Growth Strategy

Growth should be achieved through:

- Higher-value journeys
- Increased direct bookings
- Expanded accommodation partnerships
- Specialist wine tourism
- Premium destination experiences
- Strong customer retention
- International market expansion

Growth should strengthen the Go Cape Tours brand while maintaining service quality.

---

# 9. Revenue Risks

Potential commercial risks include:

- Overdependence on individual suppliers
- Seasonal demand fluctuations
- Exchange rate volatility
- Changes in international travel demand
- Supplier pricing changes
- Global economic conditions

The business should actively diversify to reduce these risks.

---

# 10. Revenue Success Measures

Commercial success should be evaluated through:

- Total revenue
- Gross profit
- Average booking value
- Revenue per customer
- Customer lifetime value
- Repeat booking rate
- Package booking ratio
- Direct booking ratio
- Supplier diversification
- International customer growth

These metrics provide a balanced view of commercial performance.

---

# 11. Long-Term Commercial Vision

The long-term objective is to establish GCT Core as the digital platform supporting a premium travel business built on expertise, curated experiences and trusted customer relationships.

Growth will be driven by delivering exceptional journeys that encourage repeat travel, customer advocacy and sustainable commercial partnerships.

Revenue should be a consequence of consistently delivering outstanding travel experiences rather than simply increasing transaction volume.

---

# 12. Conclusion

The Revenue Model defines how Go Cape Tours generates sustainable commercial value.

By focusing on premium service, curated journeys and long-term customer relationships, GCT Core positions itself as a specialist travel platform rather than a high-volume booking website.

This commercial model provides the business foundation for future pricing, financial management and platform growth.
