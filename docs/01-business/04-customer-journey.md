# GCT Core – Customer Journey

**Project:** GCT Core (Go Cape Tours Core Platform)
**Document:** Customer Journey
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 17 July 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

This document defines the complete customer journey within GCT Core.

Unlike a traditional booking website, GCT Core is designed around delivering exceptional travel experiences rather than simply selling services.

Every interaction with the customer should strengthen confidence, reduce complexity, and reinforce the premium positioning of Go Cape Tours.

The customer journey extends beyond the booking itself and encompasses the entire lifecycle, from initial inspiration through post-travel engagement.

---

# 2. Journey Philosophy

The purpose of GCT Core is not simply to process bookings.

Its purpose is to guide customers through planning, booking and experiencing extraordinary journeys throughout Southern Africa.

Technology should simplify travel while preserving the personalised service that distinguishes Go Cape Tours.

---

# 3. Customer Lifecycle

The customer journey consists of ten primary stages.

```
Discover

↓

Explore

↓

Plan

↓

Customise

↓

Book

↓

Prepare

↓

Experience

↓

Remember

↓

Share

↓

Return
```

Each stage creates opportunities for engagement and value creation.

---

# 4. Stage 1 – Discover

The customer becomes aware of Go Cape Tours.

Discovery may occur through:

- Search engines
- AI-powered search
- Social media
- Word of mouth
- Previous customers
- Travel articles
- Destination guides
- Strategic partnerships

The objective is to inspire travel rather than immediately sell.

---

# 5. Stage 2 – Explore

The customer begins exploring destinations and experiences.

Typical activities include:

- Reading destination guides
- Exploring wine regions
- Viewing curated itineraries
- Browsing accommodation
- Discovering premium experiences
- Comparing travel ideas

The platform should encourage exploration while building confidence.

---

# 6. Stage 3 – Plan

The customer begins planning a journey.

Typical considerations include:

- Travel dates
- Destinations
- Length of stay
- Budget
- Accommodation preferences
- Experience preferences
- Seasonal considerations

The platform should assist without overwhelming the customer.

---

# 7. Stage 4 – Customise

Customers personalise their journey.

Examples include:

- Selecting accommodation
- Choosing private experiences
- Adding wine experiences
- Building a bespoke itinerary
- Adjusting trip duration
- Refining destinations

This is where GCT Core differentiates itself from traditional booking platforms.

The objective is to create a journey that reflects the customer's interests rather than forcing predefined packages.

---

# 8. Stage 5 – Book

The customer confirms their itinerary.

Activities include:

- Availability verification
- Price confirmation
- Customer information
- Secure payment
- Booking confirmation

The booking experience should be simple, transparent and reassuring.

---

# 9. Stage 6 – Prepare

After booking, the platform supports the customer before arrival.

Examples include:

- Confirmation emails
- Travel documentation
- Itinerary updates
- Destination information
- Travel advice
- Weather guidance
- Packing suggestions

Preparation reduces uncertainty and improves the overall experience.

---

# 10. Stage 7 – Experience

This is the most important stage.

Customers enjoy the experiences curated by Go Cape Tours.

Examples include:

- Luxury accommodation
- Private wine experiences
- Scenic journeys
- Fine dining
- Cultural discoveries
- Wildlife encounters
- Bespoke itineraries

Technology should remain largely invisible while supporting a seamless experience.

---

# 11. Stage 8 – Remember

Following the journey, customers retain lasting memories.

The platform should encourage:

- Photo sharing
- Travel journals
- Favourite experiences
- Future travel ideas

Positive memories strengthen the Go Cape Tours brand.

---

# 12. Stage 9 – Share

Satisfied customers become brand ambassadors.

Examples include:

- Reviews
- Testimonials
- Social media posts
- Referrals
- Recommendations

This stage is a significant source of future business growth.

---

# 13. Stage 10 – Return

The customer returns for another journey.

Repeat customers may:

- Explore new destinations
- Experience different wine regions
- Book longer itineraries
- Recommend friends and family

The objective is to build lifelong relationships rather than one-time transactions.

---

# 14. Customer Touchpoints

Throughout the journey, customers interact with GCT Core through multiple touchpoints.

These include:

- Website
- Search engines
- AI assistants
- Email communication
- Booking confirmations
- Travel documentation
- Customer support
- Post-travel communication

Every interaction should reinforce trust, professionalism and premium service.

---

# 15. Customer Expectations

Customers expect:

- Accurate information
- Honest recommendations
- Premium accommodation
- Exceptional experiences
- Responsive communication
- Transparent pricing
- Reliable bookings
- Personal attention

Meeting these expectations is central to the Go Cape Tours brand.

---

# 16. Opportunities for Personalisation

GCT Core should progressively personalise the customer experience through:

- Preferred destinations
- Favourite wine styles
- Accommodation preferences
- Previous journeys
- Travel anniversaries
- Seasonal recommendations
- Curated future itineraries

Personalisation should enhance the customer experience without becoming intrusive.

---

# 17. Success Indicators

A successful customer journey is measured by:

- Booking completion rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Positive reviews
- Referral rate
- Repeat bookings
- Average itinerary value
- Customer lifetime value

These indicators help measure both commercial success and customer experience.

---

# 18. Conclusion

The customer journey defines the experience that GCT Core is designed to deliver.

Unlike traditional booking platforms that focus primarily on transactions, GCT Core is centred on helping travellers discover, plan, experience and remember extraordinary journeys.

Every business process, software component and future enhancement should contribute to delivering a seamless, premium and highly personalised travel experience that reflects the values of Go Cape Tours.
