# GCT Core – Supplier Model

**Project:** GCT Core (Go Cape Tours Core Platform)
**Document:** Supplier Model
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 17 July 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

This document defines how GCT Core interacts with external suppliers.

Suppliers provide inventory and services that form part of the customer journey, while Go Cape Tours remains responsible for designing, curating and delivering the overall travel experience.

The Supplier Model establishes the commercial relationship between GCT Core and its suppliers independently of any technical integration.

---

# 2. Supplier Philosophy

Suppliers are strategic partners that enable Go Cape Tours to deliver exceptional customer experiences.

The platform is intentionally supplier-independent.

No individual supplier should influence the architecture, business processes or customer experience of GCT Core.

Supplier integrations are implementation details, not business drivers.

---

# 3. Supplier Principles

The supplier ecosystem is governed by the following principles.

## Customer First

The customer relationship belongs to Go Cape Tours.

Customers book with Go Cape Tours, not directly with suppliers.

---

## Supplier Independence

The platform must support multiple suppliers within the same category.

Adding or replacing a supplier must not require changes to the core business model.

---

## Quality Before Quantity

Only suppliers meeting Go Cape Tours standards should be offered through the platform.

Commercial value should never outweigh customer experience.

---

## Long-Term Partnerships

The objective is to build trusted, long-term supplier relationships based on quality, reliability and mutual growth.

---

# 4. Supplier Categories

The platform supports multiple supplier categories.

## Accommodation Suppliers

Provide:

- Accommodation inventory
- Room availability
- Rates
- Images
- Property descriptions
- Policies
- Booking confirmations

Examples include:

- Hotel wholesalers
- Channel managers
- Independent properties
- Boutique hotel groups

---

## Experience Suppliers

Provide premium experiences that complement Go Cape Tours offerings.

Examples include:

- Wine estates
- Fine dining partners
- Scenic flight operators
- Marine experience providers
- Wildlife reserves
- Cultural attractions

---

## Future Supplier Categories

The platform is designed to support future integrations such as:

- Travel insurance
- Car hire
- Luxury rail
- Cruise operators
- Event providers

Future additions should not require architectural redesign.

---

# 5. Supplier Responsibilities

Suppliers are responsible for:

- Maintaining accurate inventory
- Providing current pricing
- Managing availability
- Delivering booked services
- Providing cancellation policies
- Honouring confirmed bookings

---

# 6. Go Cape Tours Responsibilities

Go Cape Tours is responsible for:

- Product selection
- Product curation
- Customer relationships
- Itinerary design
- Booking management
- Customer communication
- Quality assurance
- Payment collection
- Brand experience

---

---

# 8. Supplier Evaluation

Suppliers should be assessed against consistent criteria.

Examples include:

- Product quality
- Customer service
- Commercial terms
- Booking reliability
- Industry reputation
- Operational stability
- Geographic coverage
- Technical capability
- Brand alignment

---

# 9. Supplier Integration Principles

Supplier integrations should be:

- Modular
- Replaceable
- Scalable
- Secure
- Auditable
- Resilient

The business model must remain independent of implementation technology.

---

# 10. Commercial Relationships

Commercial agreements may include:

- Commission-based agreements
- Net rate agreements
- Contracted rates
- Preferred partnerships
- Promotional campaigns

Commercial arrangements remain confidential and should not affect the customer experience.

---

# 11. Supplier Data

Suppliers may provide:

- Product information
- Images
- Availability
- Pricing
- Policies
- Booking references
- Confirmation numbers
- Cancellation rules

Supplier data should be validated before publication.

---

# 12. Quality Assurance

GCT Core should continuously evaluate supplier performance.

Examples include:

- Customer satisfaction
- Booking accuracy
- Availability accuracy
- Response times
- Cancellation frequency
- Service consistency

Suppliers failing to meet quality standards may be suspended or removed.

---

# 13. Risk Management

The platform should minimise supplier-related risks through:

- Multiple supplier support
- Data validation
- Supplier monitoring
- Operational reporting
- Service-level expectations
- Business continuity planning

No single supplier should become a single point of failure.

---

# 14. Future Growth

The supplier ecosystem is expected to expand over time.

Future growth should support:

- Additional accommodation providers
- Premium experience partners
- Regional expansion
- New destination partnerships

Expansion should require configuration rather than architectural redesign.

---

# 15. Success Measures

The supplier ecosystem will be evaluated using:

- Supplier reliability
- Inventory quality
- Booking success rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Commercial performance
- Partner retention
- Integration stability

---

# 16. Conclusion

Suppliers enable GCT Core to deliver exceptional travel experiences, but they do not define the platform.

Go Cape Tours remains responsible for curating, managing and delivering the complete customer journey.

The Supplier Model ensures that the platform remains flexible, scalable and independent of any individual supplier while maintaining the premium standards expected of the Go Cape Tours brand.
