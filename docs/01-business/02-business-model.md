# 1. Introduction

This document defines the commercial operating model of GCT Core.

It describes how Go Cape Tours creates value for travellers, partners, suppliers, and the business itself.

Unlike a traditional tourism website, GCT Core is designed as a travel commerce platform that manages the complete customer journey, from inspiration and planning through booking, travel, and post-trip engagement.

The business model defined in this document forms the foundation for the domain model, system architecture, database design, and software implementation.

---

# 2. Business Purpose

The purpose of GCT Core is to connect travellers with carefully curated travel experiences throughout Southern Africa while delivering exceptional service through technology and local expertise.

The platform enables customers to discover, customise, purchase, and manage complete travel itineraries from a single destination.

# 9. Product Strategy

Every product offered through GCT Core should satisfy at least one of the following criteria:

- Premium quality.
- Authentic local experience.
- Exceptional customer reviews.
- Reliable supplier.
- Commercial sustainability.
- Alignment with the Go Cape Tours brand.

The platform prioritises quality over quantity.

# 14. Long-Term Business Vision

The long-term objective is to establish GCT Core as the digital commerce platform that powers the continued growth of Go Cape Tours.

The platform will expand from premium touring into a fully integrated travel ecosystem supporting accommodation, experiences, transport, dynamic packaging, and future travel services while preserving the personalised service that defines the Go Cape Tours brand.

The business model is intentionally designed to support future growth without requiring fundamental architectural redesign.
