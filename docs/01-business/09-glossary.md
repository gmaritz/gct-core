# GCT Core – Business Glossary

**Project:** GCT Core (Go Cape Tours Core Platform)  
**Document:** Business Glossary  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 17 July 2026

---

# 1. Introduction

This glossary defines the standard business terminology used throughout GCT Core.

It establishes a single, shared language for business stakeholders, developers, designers, partners and documentation.

Each term has one authoritative meaning within the platform.

---

# A

## Accommodation

A place where a traveller stays during a journey.

Examples include hotels, boutique hotels, guest houses, lodges, villas and apartments.

Accommodation may be booked independently or as part of an itinerary.

---

# B

## Booking

The commercial confirmation of an itinerary.

A booking records the customer's commitment to purchase one or more services.

---

## Booking Engine

The business capability responsible for creating, confirming, managing and fulfilling bookings.

---

## Business Rule

A permanent rule describing how the business operates, independent of technology.

---

# C

## Curated Experience

A premium experience selected or designed by Go Cape Tours because it meets defined quality standards.

---

## Customer

An individual or organisation purchasing services through Go Cape Tours.

---

# D

## Destination

A geographical location visited during a journey.

Examples include Cape Town, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and the Garden Route.

---

## Domain Model

The conceptual representation of the business used to design the software platform.

---

# E

## Experience

A premium tourism activity forming part of a customer's journey.

Examples include:

- Wine experiences
- Culinary experiences
- Safari experiences
- Scenic experiences
- Cultural experiences

Experiences represent the core commercial offering of Go Cape Tours.

---

# G

## GCT Core

The travel commerce platform that powers Go Cape Tours.

It manages journeys, itineraries, bookings, suppliers, customers and related business operations.

---

## Go Cape Tours

The premium travel brand established in 2002.

Go Cape Tours owns the customer relationship and delivers curated travel experiences.

---

# I

## Itinerary

The planned sequence of accommodation and experiences making up a journey.

An itinerary may exist before a booking is confirmed.

---

# J

## Journey

The complete travel experience undertaken by a traveller.

A journey begins during planning and concludes after the traveller returns home.

The journey is the highest-level business concept within GCT Core.

---

# P

## Package

A commercially defined offering combining multiple services into a single product.

A package may include accommodation and curated experiences.

Packages are reusable products.

---

## Platform

The complete software ecosystem supporting Go Cape Tours.

The platform extends beyond the website and includes business services, integrations, administration and customer management.

---

## Premium Experience

A curated service that reflects the quality standards and positioning of Go Cape Tours.

---

# R

## Revenue

Income generated through delivering premium travel experiences and related services.

---

# S

## Service

A non-physical commercial offering delivered to the traveller.

Examples include:

- Private wine experience
- Bespoke itinerary planning
- Guided experiences

Unlike products in traditional e-commerce, services are delivered over time.

---

## Supplier

An external organisation providing accommodation or other services that form part of the customer journey.

Suppliers enable the journey but do not own the customer relationship.

---

# T

## Traveller

The individual undertaking the journey.

The traveller may or may not be the person making the booking.

---

# U

## Ubiquitous Language

The shared vocabulary used consistently throughout GCT Core.

Every business concept should be described using the terminology defined in this glossary.

---

# V

## Value Proposition

The unique value Go Cape Tours delivers through expertise, personalised service and curated premium journeys.

---

# W

## Workflow

A defined sequence of business activities performed to achieve a specific outcome.

Examples include the booking lifecycle and supplier onboarding.

---

# Glossary Principles

The glossary is governed by the following principles:

- Every important business concept has one authoritative definition.
- The same term should not have multiple meanings.
- Different terms should not describe the same concept.
- Definitions should reflect the business rather than technical implementation.
- The glossary should evolve as the platform evolves.

---

# Conclusion

The Business Glossary defines the common language of GCT Core.

All future business documentation, domain models, APIs, database designs, software components and user interfaces should use these definitions consistently.

Maintaining a shared vocabulary ensures clear communication, reduces ambiguity and strengthens the long-term maintainability of the platform.
