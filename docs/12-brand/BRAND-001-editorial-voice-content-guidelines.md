# Purpose

This document defines the editorial voice, language, vocabulary and content standards for Go Cape Tours.

It establishes a consistent style for every customer-facing interaction, including:

- Homepage
- Journey pages
- Destination pages
- Accommodation descriptions
- Booking flow
- Confirmation emails
- Marketing campaigns
- Social media
- AI-generated content
- Future content management systems

The objective is to ensure every piece of communication reflects the same premium, curated brand experience.

---

# Brand Philosophy

Go Cape Tours is not a travel marketplace.

It is a curator of exceptional private journeys.

The platform exists to help guests discover experiences worth taking rather than presenting every available option.

---

# Brand Promise

> **We don't present every possible journey—we present the journeys worth taking.**

This statement shall guide all editorial decisions.

---

# Editorial Principles

Every piece of content should communicate:

- Confidence
- Authenticity
- Expertise
- Warmth
- Elegance
- Simplicity
- Curation

The tone should always reassure rather than overwhelm.

---

# Tone of Voice

The voice of Go Cape Tours is:

- Calm
- Refined
- Knowledgeable
- Welcoming
- Personal
- Trustworthy

The tone is never:

- Aggressive
- Pushy
- Loud
- Overly promotional
- Sales driven

---

# Writing Style

Write in a conversational but polished manner.

Prefer:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear language
- Active voice
- Positive phrasing
- Descriptive storytelling

Avoid unnecessary adjectives or exaggerated marketing language.

---

# Preferred Vocabulary

Preferred terms include:

- Journey
- Curated
- Private
- Discover
- Explore
- Experience
- Collection
- Exceptional
- Authentic
- Boutique
- Scenic
- Local
- Carefully selected
- Thoughtfully designed
- Handpicked
- Tailored

---

# Vocabulary to Avoid

Avoid words that diminish the premium positioning.

Examples:

- Cheap
- Discount
- Bargain
- Lowest price
- Mass market
- Generic
- Standard package
- Budget deal
- Flash sale

Where commercial information is required, present it with restraint.

---

# Journey Terminology

Use:

- Journey
- Experience
- Collection
- Escape
- Discovery
- Expedition
- Itinerary

Avoid using "Tour" repeatedly where a richer term is appropriate.

The company name remains **Go Cape Tours**, but the editorial language should favour "journey" and "experience".

---

# Pricing Language

Price supports the journey.

It does not dominate the presentation.

Preferred examples:

```
From R18 950 per couple
```

```
Includes boutique accommodation and private guiding
```

Savings may be displayed but should remain visually and editorially understated.

Example:

```
Save 15%
```

Never allow savings to become the primary message.

---

# Calls to Action

Preferred CTAs:

- Explore Experiences
- View Journey
- Plan Your Journey
- Discover More
- Start Exploring
- View Collection

Avoid:

- Buy Now
- Book Fast
- Hurry
- Limited Time Only
- Click Here

Urgency should come from the quality and availability of the experience rather than aggressive wording.

---

# Editorial Hierarchy

Content should generally follow this structure:

1. Inspiration
2. Context
3. Experience
4. Practical Information
5. Call to Action

Lead with emotion before detail.

---

# Accommodation Content

Accommodation supports the journey.

It should be described in terms of how it enhances the overall experience.

Example:

> "A boutique vineyard retreat offering tranquil surroundings and exceptional hospitality."

Avoid listing facilities before establishing atmosphere.

---

# Journey Highlights

Highlights should reinforce quality rather than quantity.

Examples:

- Private guide
- Boutique accommodation
- Scenic coastal drives
- Award-winning wine estates
- Gourmet dining
- Local expertise
- Carefully selected experiences

---

# Imagery Principles

Photography should feel:

- Authentic
- Cinematic
- Elegant
- Natural
- Aspirational

Avoid:

- Generic stock imagery
- Overly staged scenes
- Excessive text overlays
- Busy collages

Images should invite the visitor into the journey.

---

# Personalisation

Future personalised content shall remain editorially consistent.

Recommendations should be phrased as guidance rather than sales.

Example:

> "Based on your interests, we think you'll enjoy these curated journeys."

---

# AI Content Standards

Any AI-generated content shall:

- Follow this editorial guide.
- Preserve the Go Cape Tours voice.
- Prioritise clarity over creativity.
- Never invent factual information.
- Avoid repetitive wording.
- Maintain the same premium positioning as human-authored content.

Human review is recommended before publication.

---

# Accessibility

Editorial content shall:

- Use clear, readable language.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Support inclusive communication.
- Maintain descriptive link text.
- Use meaningful headings.

---

# Governance

All customer-facing content shall comply with BRAND-001.

New content types should be reviewed against these standards before publication.

Where uncertainty exists, favour simplicity, authenticity and clarity.

---

# Current Reference Implementation

BRAND-001 is based on the verified implementation of:

- Homepage Showcase
- Editorial Panel
- Curated Journey Card
- Homepage Merchandising
- View Model Library

These implementations establish the baseline editorial style for the platform.

---

# Brand Evolution

The editorial voice may evolve over time, but changes should preserve the core principles of:

- Curation
- Authenticity
- Expertise
- Warmth
- Elegance

Major revisions shall be versioned and reviewed.
