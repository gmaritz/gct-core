# Objective

Implement the Homepage Showcase component.

The Homepage Showcase replaces the traditional Hero section and serves as the primary merchandising surface of the Go Cape Tours website.

It combines brand storytelling with dynamic merchandising by presenting carefully curated featured journeys generated by the GCT Core merchandising engine.

The Homepage Showcase shall consume the DS-001 Design System and Design Tokens.

No direct supplier integration shall occur within this milestone.

---

# Business Purpose

The Homepage Showcase exists to:

- Inspire visitors.
- Promote premium private travel.
- Create urgency through featured offers.
- Highlight exceptional value.
- Encourage immediate enquiries and bookings.

The Homepage Showcase is not a catalogue.

The Homepage Showcase is not a search interface.

It is a premium editorial presentation of Go Cape Tours' most compelling current journeys.

# Engineering Principle

The Homepage Showcase shall not communicate directly with Hotelbeds.

Future architecture

```
Hotelbeds

↓

Accommodation Engine

↓

Package Builder

↓

Business Rules

↓

Merchandising Engine

↓

Homepage View Model

↓

Homepage Showcase
```

The Homepage Showcase renders only the supplied view model.

It has no knowledge of supplier integrations.

---

# Responsive Behaviour
