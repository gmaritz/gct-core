## 3. Application money representation

**Decision 1A — approved by the Project Owner:** use an immutable Domain Money value object with an exact signed base-10 decimal amount and explicit currency. Encapsulate `decimal.js` within the Domain implementation, use exact decimal arithmetic without JavaScript `number` for authoritative monetary calculations, and serialize monetary values as canonical decimal strings. The proposed uppercase three-letter currency validation and encoding rules below remain subject to specification approval. Integer minor units alone simplify settlement but cannot preserve supplier sub-minor prices; an exact decimal supports both. A decimal string alone serialises safely but still needs exact arithmetic. This decision does not itself authorise implementation or approve the complete specification.

**Project Owner-approved final commercial precision principle (2026-11-20):** All final GCT commercial monetary amounts SHALL be expressed to two decimal places at explicitly defined commercial rounding boundaries. This includes customer-facing GCT prices and contractual amounts such as accommodation selling amounts, package totals, Reservation totals, initial payments and future balances. Internal calculations SHALL retain exact decimal precision until the applicable approved boundary; they SHALL NOT be prematurely rounded to two decimal places. The two-decimal rule defines final commercial amount precision, not the precision of supplier source values, FX rates or intermediate calculations. The applicable rounding mode, point of rounding and line/stop/total reconciliation must be specified by the owning commercial policy. Existing approved rules, including INT-004 `ROUND_HALF_UP`, remain authoritative and must not be changed by this principle. This approval does not itself authorise implementation or approve the complete specification.

1. **Decision 1B — conditional:** `Decimal(19,4)`, with at most 15 integral digits and four fractional digits, remains a candidate only for specifically bounded persisted monetary columns, not an approved universal limit. It matches existing Payment, Refund and PaymentAttempt amount columns; it does **not** establish the required storage contract for every other financial value or preserve arbitrary supplier/intermediate precision. The 2026-11-20 investigation, [SPEC-050-INV-002](../../08-architecture/investigations/SPEC-050-INV-002-decision-1b-evidence-report.md), found no authoritative Hotelbeds-wide maximum amount, integral-digit limit, fractional scale or wire-format guarantee, and no generic GCT maximum for accommodation stops, rooms, nights, adults or children. It verified a Cape Winelands-specific maximum of 16 selected room-rate inputs across two stops, but no supplier amount or FX maximum; therefore no maximum quote amount follows. Approval of any persisted range requires explicit category-specific acceptance bounds, validated application aggregation/resource limits and exact persistence/reconstruction evidence. For any column whose approved contract imposes a bound, values outside it SHALL be rejected at that boundary, not rounded or truncated; a wider requirement needs an approved affected-specification and storage change.
2. The canonical decimal text SHALL match `-?(0|[1-9][0-9]*)(\.[0-9]*[1-9])?`, with zero written `0`, not `-0`; exponent notation, plus signs, whitespace, separators, leading zeroes, trailing fractional zeroes, NaN and infinity are invalid. This is a value encoding, not a display format. For example `12.3400` from an exact database decimal normalises to `12.34`, and `0.0000` to `0`. External source text may be parsed by a source-specific exact grammar and normalised without loss; reject invalid or unsupported source values.
3. Value equality SHALL compare exact numeric value and currency, not text scale. Currency SHALL be validated against a versioned supported-currency/precision policy; unknown or absent codes fail explicitly. Counts and dates may use integer types, but monetary values, percentages and exchange rates used in financial calculation SHALL not pass through binary floating-point arithmetic.
4. Exact intermediate calculations SHALL use integer coefficients/scales or exact rational arithmetic; round only at an approved boundary. Input digit/scale limits and computation resource limits SHALL be checked before exponentiation or allocation. Exceeding supported limits is an explicit error, never a partial amount. Field-specific non-negative, positive and zero constraints belong to the owning contract; signed values may represent an authorised adjustment, not an automatic permission for negative Payment or Refund.
5. Under approved Decision 2, ZAR has two minor-unit digits and EUR has two minor-unit digits at settlement boundaries. ZAR is the sole GCT commercial currency for customer quotations, Reservations and contractual amounts, including in production. Development and testing must support ZAR and EUR supplier input; EUR exercises the configured EUR-to-ZAR conversion path. In production Hotelbeds is expected to provide ZAR prices, and EUR supplier input is not a production commercial requirement. Do not enable additional supplier currencies. ZAR is the only supported PayFast transaction and refund currency in testing and production. Preserve exact supplier source amounts and exact FX rates independently of final commercial precision, using the approved exact-decimal Money architecture and rounding only at explicitly authorised boundaries. All final GCT commercial amounts use two decimal places under the Project Owner-approved principle above. Four fractional digits are not an approved universal supplier or intermediate limit. Currency minor units, supplier precision, internal calculation precision, final commercial precision, display formatting and database scale are separate constraints.

`decimal.js` arithmetic uses configured significant-digit precision and can round operation results to that precision. The Domain implementation SHALL configure precision explicitly and validate input digit/scale and computation limits before calculation. The configured precision SHALL be sufficient for every permitted intermediate operation, including aggregation and multiplication, so that no unintended intermediate rounding occurs. If exactness cannot be guaranteed within the supported limits, the calculation SHALL fail explicitly rather than return an approximation. A non-terminating division SHALL remain exact through rational arithmetic until an approved rounding boundary, or fail explicitly if unsupported. Rounding SHALL occur only at approved boundaries and SHALL NOT use binary floating-point monetary calculations.

## 4. Arithmetic, rounding and conversion

Addition, subtraction and comparisons SHALL require identical currencies. Multiplication by a quantity, percentage or FX rate SHALL use its exact integer/decimal representation. Preserve the declared pricing basis: multiply a per-room or per-person price only by its applicable count; sum distinct room rates to the stop total and distinct stop lines to the package total exactly once. A group-total GCT line SHALL not be multiplied by participant count again. No duplicate or missing stop or currency mismatch may be silently accepted.

All final GCT commercial monetary amounts SHALL be expressed to two decimal places, as approved by the Project Owner on 2026-11-20. Exact internal values SHALL be retained until a named commercial rounding boundary; two-decimal final precision does not authorise rounding supplier source values, FX rates or intermediate calculations. An approved policy SHALL specify the input, output scale (two decimal places for final GCT commercial amounts), rounding mode, exact point of rounding, and reconciliation of independently rounded lines to totals. Storage or deserialisation SHALL never round. For otherwise undecided calculations, recommend `ROUND_HALF_EVEN` at the final named commercial boundary, with no intermediate rounding; this is **not approved** and must not override an established commercial or supplier rule. Pricing/Finance and the affected specification owners must decide line-versus-total boundaries, permitted differences and allocation; do not invent a balancing adjustment. The existing INT-004 `ROUND_HALF_UP` rule in §4 remains unchanged and authoritative for its defined initial-payment calculation.

**Existing specific rule:** [INT-004 v1.0.0 §5.1](../../08-integration/INT-004-cape-winelands-dynamic-availability-pricing.md) is Architect Approved for the Signature Cape Winelands initial requirement. For booking more than 14 calendar days before departure, apply the effective configured rate to the GCT group component, independently calculate its complement, round each `ROUND_HALF_UP` to ZAR cents, add all final accommodation stop lines to the initial amount and require initial plus future balance to reconcile to the quote total. If they do not reconcile, fail closed. At 14 days or fewer the initial amount is the total. Preserve the approved timezone, effective policy version, rates, thresholds and due-date rules. Do not substitute half-even or change the split to force reconciliation. For example, where the GCT group component is ZAR `100.01`, a 50/50 split using `ROUND_HALF_UP` produces an initial GCT component of `50.01` and a future GCT component of `50.01`. Their combined amount is `100.02`, so the calculation fails reconciliation rather than inventing a one-cent adjustment. Accommodation amounts remain separate and are added according to the approved initial-payment calculation.

Conversion SHALL occur only under an explicitly authorised pricing policy, never in Payment or the PayFast adapter or while loading historical values. Retain exact source amount/currency, target amount/currency, exact rate and direction, policy/source identity and version, effective time and conversion/rounding boundary, alongside relevant quote provenance. A conversion SHALL multiply exact source by an exact positive rate, then round only as that policy authorises. Same-currency amounts require no FX; unsupported pairs, missing policy/rate or stale/invalid provenance fail closed. [CUR-002](../../07-application/implementations/pricing/CUR-002-configurable-accommodation-currency-reconciliation.md) documents a configured EUR-to-ZAR path and [CUR-002.1](../../07-application/implementations/pricing/CUR-002.1-persist-accommodation-currency-conversion-metadata.md) its snapshot metadata; do not treat its example rate or current numeric implementation as a universal FX policy. Pricing/Finance must approve precision, rate provenance, timing and rounding for each supported policy.

## 5. JSON, snapshots and provider boundaries

Proposed versioned money JSON shape: `{"amount":"123.45","currency":"ZAR"}`. The amount is canonical decimal text under section 3; currency is the validated code. Canonical decimal text may omit insignificant trailing zeroes, so it is a value representation rather than a fixed-width display format. Final GCT commercial values represented by these snapshots SHALL have a numeric value exactly rounded to two decimal places at the owning approved commercial boundary; customer-facing and settlement formatting SHALL render exactly two fractional digits. Each owning snapshot schema SHALL define its version, field location, currency rules, required/optional fields and explicit decoding of each supported historical version. Decimal rates SHALL also be strings with a separately defined exact rate schema. A schema version identifies the encoding; readers SHALL reject unknown versions and malformed values, rather than default missing amounts to zero. JSON numbers SHALL NOT represent new authoritative financial values.

Reservation snapshots SHALL carry separate total, agreed initial amount, future balance and due date, received and outstanding values. A snapshot of a supplier price SHALL retain original amount, currency and basis and, where conversion occurred, its separately preserved conversion information. Payment and Refund snapshots SHALL preserve their own amount and currency and sufficient historical precision. Versioning and exact field placement require APP-004.2, APP-006.2 and physical-model approval; this proposal creates no JSON field.

At the initial PayFast boundary, accept only an approved positive ZAR transaction amount exactly expressible in cents; extra **non-zero** fractional digits are invalid, not rounded. Format accepted values with exactly two fractional digits for checkout and compare the verified ITN gross to the persisted attempt in exact cents. For example `12.3` formats as `12.30`, while `12.345` is rejected. Preserve validation and evidence separately from lifecycle transitions. A provider constraint is not a universal GCT currency rule; no provider conversion is implied.

## 6. Persistence and reconstruction

For existing `Decimal(19,4)` Payment, Refund and PaymentAttempt amount columns, the proposed mapping SHALL validate range and scale **before** database assignment, create the Prisma Decimal from exact decimal text, and restore it from an exact decimal representation without `Number`, `parseFloat` or floating-point arithmetic. Where these records represent final ZAR transaction/refund amounts, their accepted monetary value SHALL be exactly expressible to two decimal places; excess non-zero fractional digits SHALL be rejected rather than silently rounded. This two-decimal rule does not resolve the maximum amount or prove that the current column and application mapping provide an exact round trip. Currency codes SHALL map to and from the approved currency reference without fallback or invented defaults. For a stored `999999999999999.9999`, the restored value and currency must equal the input exactly; `1000000000000000` or `0.00001` must fail before persistence to these columns. These examples do not establish a universal money bound. Non-significant trailing zeroes may be removed; the numeric value must not change.

For approved JSON/JSONB snapshots, write and read the versioned string representation without a numeric intermediate. For any other relational column or provider evidence, its owner SHALL specify an exact compatible mapping and bounds before use; incompatible storage rejects the value. Mappers and repositories SHALL surface malformed, missing, overflowed and unsupported data as explicit errors with appropriate operational reporting, not as empty or zero-valued successful reconstructions. Read/write tests must demonstrate application → database → application equality for every applicable amount and currency, including complete owned Refunds when Payment reconstruction is separately authorised.

This contract does not approve any new JSON structure, schema or repository API. [SPEC-027 v3.0.0](SPEC-027-physical-data-model-v3.0.0.md) proposes equivalent `Decimal(19,4)` handling for existing Payment/Refund columns but remains Draft; APP-004, APP-005, APP-006 and SPEC-026–029 require coordinated amendments and approval.

## 7. Legacy compatibility and migration

Current code already passes financial values through JavaScript `number`, and Reservation JSON contains numeric values. Their original source precision cannot generally be recovered by converting the resulting number to text. A decimal column may preserve its stored value exactly without proving that upstream input was accurate. Historical numeric JSON tokens, even if parsed losslessly from raw stored text, establish only the stored decimal, not the original supplier or commercial intent.

Before any implementation, the responsible owners SHALL approve an inventory and classification of affected source, snapshot, Payment, Refund and Attempt records and consumers; a version-aware read/write and rollout strategy; evidence-based conversion rules; reconciliation and exception ownership; backward compatibility; rollback and verification. An exact historic value may be migrated only when authoritative evidence and compatible identity establish it. Ambiguous, conflicting, unsupported or unverifiable data SHALL remain available for controlled investigation, not be silently rounded, fabricated, zero-filled or represented as a verified canonical amount. Preserve raw evidence, original record identities and history subject to approved retention/access controls. D10 does not permit manufacturing historical Payments or replacements.

Legacy readers and migration tooling SHALL not claim a precision guarantee before a record has passed the approved evidence-based classification. No live database operation, backfill, schema change or deployment is authorised by this draft.

## 8. Required validation and acceptance criteria

After decisions and controlled amendments, a separately authorised implementation is acceptable only if focused tests demonstrate:

1. Exact parsing, canonical serialisation and currency validation; numeric equality across trailing-zero scales; rejection of invalid strings, unknown currencies, negative/zero values where prohibited, excess non-zero precision, range overflow and resource limits.
2. Exact addition, subtraction, comparison and multiplication, with mixed currencies rejected; per-room, per-stop and group basis respected, no duplicated accommodation and no extra 10% margin.
3. Exact Signature Cape Winelands approved half-up split, boundary dates, due date, effective version, full-payment path and the half-cent reconciliation failure above; other policy rounding only after approval.
4. Explicit approved FX provenance and exact conversion; same-currency/no-conversion path; missing, unsupported or invalid rates and currencies fail closed; source amount remains distinct from converted amount.
5. Versioned Reservation and Payment JSON encode/decode and actual database round trips retain all accepted values and currencies, including boundary values, zeroes, selected supplier prices, totals, initial/future terms, received/outstanding, PaymentAttempt and each complete Refund; no binary conversion or silent rounding occurs.
6. PayFast exact-cent formatting and gross-to-attempt comparison reject mismatch and excess precision; verified provider evidence is not treated as an approved canonical transition or Reservation confirmation.
7. Legacy numeric, invalid, unknown-version and incomplete records follow approved classification and explicit error/recovery paths; no invented history or successful reconstruction from defaults.
8. The implementation meets existing applicable regressions and approved migration/rollback tests. A mocked Prisma round trip alone does not demonstrate database precision or a complete Aggregate reconstruction.
9. Explicit `decimal.js` precision and validated limits preserve exact permitted intermediates, including aggregation and multiplication; insufficient precision or unsupported computation fails explicitly, and non-terminating division is not silently rounded before an approved boundary.
10. ZAR-only commercial amounts and PayFast transactions and refunds, ZAR and EUR supplier input in development and testing, and the configured EUR-to-ZAR test path respect Decision 2; additional supplier currencies are not enabled, and Payment and the PayFast adapter do not convert currencies.
